3rd Order Planner
Jerk-limited planning of feeds and rapids as one continuous motion, corners blended within the Corner Tolerance (G64 Px, G61 and G61.1 are honoured). Needs Actuator Space Limits, which hold the jerk limits.
The planned curves are followed exactly on every servo cycle instead of as short straight segments. Needs KFLOP or Kogna firmware 5.5.1 or later.
Velocity, acceleration and jerk limits per motor (the table below) instead of per CAD axis, applied through the kinematics. The feed rate and the tolerances stay in CAD space.
Writes every planned segment to /tmp/TPSegLog.csv and, with the 3rd Order Planner, the timeline to /tmp/TP3_Timeline.log (c:\Temp on Windows) for the MotionLogPlotter. Leave off in normal use.
